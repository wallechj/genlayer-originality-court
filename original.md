# The Clockmaker's Daughter

Mara was the clockmaker's daughter, and the town of Bellwick trusted her with its time.

Her father Eldric had built the great clock above the market square, and when his hands began to shake, Mara took over the winding. She oiled the brass gears with lavender oil, the way her father taught her, and she listened for the small click that meant the mechanism was happy.

One autumn morning the clock stopped. The townspeople gathered below, whispering, because the clock had not struck thirteen in forty years, and Mara intended to keep it that way. She climbed the tower alone, lantern in hand, and found a single gear tooth snapped clean off.

She filed a new tooth from a brass spoon her father had given her, fitted it by candlelight, and wound the clock three slow turns. When the clock struck twelve, the whole town held its breath, and then the bells rang true.
